# Marketing / Business / Decision-Making Implementation Prompt

Implement production Marketing, Business Strategy and Decision-Making capabilities for zanything.

## Marketing
- Audience/segment model.
- Positioning and value proposition.
- Campaign/content workflow.
- Channel strategy and experiment design.
- KPI/attribution model.
- Explicit assumptions vs evidence.

## Business
- Market/problem/value proposition analysis.
- Competitive alternatives.
- Unit economics/cost/revenue assumptions.
- Operating model and risk register.
- Scenario/sensitivity analysis.

## Decision Making
- Explicit decision criteria.
- Weighting aligned to user goal.
- Alternatives and trade-offs.
- Recommendation with reversibility and risk.
- Explain what evidence would change the recommendation.

## Production requirements
- Persist decision inputs and evidence provenance when configured.
- Never fabricate market statistics, testimonials or financial data.
- Mark estimates and assumptions clearly.
- Support export to reports, spreadsheets and presentations.

## Tests
- criteria weighting
- deterministic matrix calculations
- missing-evidence handling
- scenario analysis
- provenance preservation
- no fabricated-data regressions

Completion requires implementation, tests, docs, metrics/evals and release evidence.
