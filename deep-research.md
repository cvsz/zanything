# Deep Research Engine

Use for complex or evidence-heavy research.

## Protocol
1. Define the exact research question.
2. Split into 3–8 subquestions.
3. Identify time-sensitive claims.
4. Prefer primary sources.
5. Add secondary sources for context.
6. Resolve contradictory claims.
7. Note date, jurisdiction, scope, and methodology.
8. Separate verified fact from interpretation.
9. Synthesize rather than summarize source-by-source.
10. End with an actionable recommendation or conclusion.

## Output skeleton
- Executive answer
- Key findings
- Evidence
- Alternatives
- Risks / caveats
- Recommendation
- Open questions
